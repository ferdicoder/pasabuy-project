# PasaBUY: Ecommerce for Travellers within the Philippines

## About: 
- PasaBuy is a web-based matching marketplace for travellers that wants to establish a business and hop city to city at the same time, within the Philippines. Features such as matching based on route of the seller and request-first marketplace for the buyer makes PasaBuy ideal for travelling nomads or for the people with on-going trips. 

## Latest Features: 
- Login & Registration - account user creation and registration, you don't need a role just create an account then create trips or request a product
- Trip Planning - sets travel itinerary including destinations, capacity for goods, and date of arrival
- Request Creation - posting a product by defining the image, product name, estimated price, and your location
- Request-Trip Marketplace - request and trips as the user's product & services, for browsing and searching
- Compatibility Matching  - the main feed define and filters the top match for your request or planned trips
